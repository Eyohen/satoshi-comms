FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

Do I need a website before I work with you?

No. Many businesses start with only an Instagram page or a WhatsApp catalogue. We build from wherever you are.

What do you mean by a custom platform?

Software built around your daily work. It could be an order tracker for a fashion house, a booking system for a salon, or a dashboard that shows sales and stock in one place.

Are the businesses in Our Work real clients?

Two of them are. Pamper And Fun was a recent client project; what you see there is a concept preview we built ourselves, not a live production app. Fashion OMS is an active client, so their real screens stay private; anything we show for it would be a concept recreation too. The Before and After grid is sample work we created to show our design thinking across different industries and languages.

How long does a project take?

A website usually takes two to four weeks. A platform depends on what it needs to do, and you'll get a timeline in the plan.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what we build. After the first chat you get a fixed quote, so there are no surprises.

Do you work outside Nigeria?

Yes. We work with businesses in Nigeria, the UK, Europe and beyond, in four languages.
