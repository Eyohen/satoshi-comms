About

A small studio in Lagos,
building for the world.

We design websites and build custom platforms for small businesses that want to look better, work smarter and grow without chaos.

Why we exist

Most small businesses are run on grit, WhatsApp and a notebook.

That works, up to a point. Then orders get missed, customers can't find you online, and the owner becomes the only person who knows where anything stands.

Satoshi Comms exists to fix that. We build the website that brings customers in and the platform that keeps the work organised once they arrive. Everything is shaped around how the business already runs, not around a template.

We're based in Lagos and work with businesses in Nigeria, the UK and across Europe, in English, French, German and Italian.

Proof from a real project

We build like it has to work on day one.

Pamper And Fun is a booking site and staff platform we designed and built for a client in beauty and wellness. Every choice on show there, from how booking works to what staff see first thing in the morning, is a real decision we made for a real business, not a mockup.

Process

From first chat to launch in four steps.

No jargon and no surprises. At every step you know what you're getting and what comes next.

  1. 1

    Tell us how you work.

    We talk through your business, your customers and what's slowing you down.

    You getA clear picture of what you actually need
  2. 2

    We map it out.

    You get a written plan: what we'll build, how long it takes and what it costs.

    You getA plan and a fixed quote
  3. 3

    We design and build.

    You see the design first, then progress as we build, and you shape it with us along the way.

    You getDesigns to review, then a working build
  4. 4

    Launch and grow.

    We go live, train your team if needed, and stay on hand for what comes next.

    You getA live site or platform, and support
